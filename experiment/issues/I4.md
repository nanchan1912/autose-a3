Our pages have a few headings like "Changelog" or "Edit this page" that we don't want listed in the generated table of contents, but they should otherwise stay normal headings (people link to them, and we use `permalink=True`).

Proposal: with `attr_list` enabled, a heading that has the class `no-toc`

```markdown
## Changelog {: .no-toc }
```

is not included in `md.toc` or `md.toc_tokens`. Everything else about that heading should stay as it is today: it still gets its `id`, anchor link and permalink, and the ids of the other headings must stay unique and unchanged. A heading can of course have other classes as well (`{: .fancy .no-toc }`). Headings without the class are not affected.
